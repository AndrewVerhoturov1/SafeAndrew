# POLG GeneReviews 2024 — neurogastrointestinal phenotype — русский разбор

## 1. POLG can link neuropathy and GI disease

POLG spectrum includes:

- sensory neuronopathy/ganglionopathy;
- axonal sensorimotor neuropathy;
- ataxia;
- ptosis/external ophthalmoplegia;
- seizures/other CNS features;
- GI dysmotility in selected phenotypes.

## 2. MNGIE-like without classic TYMP MRI

POLG-related neurogastrointestinal disease can lack diffuse leukoencephalopathy.

Therefore absence of classic TYMP MRI pattern does **not** close mitochondrial neuro-GI branch.

## 3. Phenotype should remain multisystem

POLG becomes more plausible when neuropathy/GI disease coexist with combinations of:

- ataxia;
- ophthalmoplegia/ptosis;
- epilepsy/migraine-like/CNS features;
- liver vulnerability;
- hearing/other mitochondrial signs.

None is individually required for all POLG phenotypes.

## 4. Testing

Broad genetic testing must include adequate POLG coverage and correct inheritance/variant interpretation.

## 5. Safety boundary

GeneReviews contains medication-specific cautions in confirmed/suspected POLG disease. Agent must not alter therapy based only on unconfirmed genetic suspicion.
