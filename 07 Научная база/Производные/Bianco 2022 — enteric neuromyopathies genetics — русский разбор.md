# Bianco 2022 — enteric neuromyopathies genetics — русский разбор

## 1. CIPO is a final syndrome, not a mechanism

Severe dysmotility can arise from abnormalities of:

- enteric neurons/glia;
- extrinsic innervation;
- interstitial cells of Cajal;
- smooth muscle;
- mitochondrial/systemic pathways.

## 2. Genetic strategy must follow compartment

If pathology/clinical phenotype looks **myopathic**, prioritize smooth-muscle genes.

If **neuropathic/systemic**, prioritize enteric-neural, mitochondrial and multisystem genetic causes.

## 3. Why pathology matters

Same radiological CIPO phenotype can result from different microscopic mechanisms.

Full-thickness pathology, when reliable tissue exists, may turn genetic testing from a very broad list into a mechanistically focused strategy.

## 4. Avoid single-gene anchoring

Review covers multiple rare genetic forms; no single gene explains all adult CIPO.

## 5. SafeAndrew implication

Genetic report should classify whether candidate variant plausibly explains:

- GI phenotype only;
- peripheral neuropathy only;
- or both through one known multisystem mechanism.
