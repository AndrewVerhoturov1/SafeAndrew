# ACTG2 Visceral Myopathy — русский разбор

## 1. Core mechanism

ACTG2 disease is a **smooth-muscle visceral myopathy**, primarily GI + urinary tract.

Phenotype can include:

- chronic intestinal pseudo-obstruction;
- abdominal distention/functional obstruction;
- neonatal severe hypoperistalsis at one end;
- milder intermittent adult presentations at the other;
- megacystis/urinary dysfunction.

## 2. Family history can be negative

Many pathogenic variants are de novo, so absence of affected relatives does not exclude ACTG2.

## 3. Critical unification question

ACTG2 is strong for a visceral myopathy phenotype but does not usually explain generalized peripheral neuropathy.

Therefore a positive ACTG2 result could mean:

- GI disease explained, neurologic disease separate;
- or a more complex blended diagnosis.

Agent must not force one-gene unification when known phenotype does not support it.

## 4. When to prioritize

More relevant if:

- bladder/megacystis phenotype;
- pathology supports smooth-muscle myopathy;
- longstanding nonmechanical obstruction pattern;
- family/de novo compatible presentation.
