# RFC1 GeneReviews 2026 — русский разбор

## 1. Why RFC1 matters

RFC1 expansion is a common cause of **late-onset** sensory neuropathy/ataxia in appropriate populations and may initially look like acquired sensory neuronopathy.

Possible features:

- sensory neuronopathy;
- gait/sensory ataxia;
- bilateral vestibular dysfunction;
- cerebellar signs;
- chronic cough;
- autonomic dysfunction;
- posterior-column spinal abnormalities.

## 2. Technology trap

Most disease is caused by biallelic **intronic repeat expansions**.

Standard exome or sequence-only panel can miss this mechanism.

Request must explicitly state whether RFC1 repeat-expansion testing is included.

## 3. Fit boundary

Classic RFC1 disease is usually later onset; severe CIPO is not a defining phenotype.

Therefore RFC1 is especially useful if expert review supports sensory neuronopathy/ataxia and standard genetics is unrevealing, but it is not automatically the best unifying neuro-GI diagnosis.

## 4. Acquired-mimic warning

GeneReviews notes RFC1 expansions have been found in patients initially assigned acquired sensory-neuronopathy labels.

Thus an atypical course/poor fit to immune explanation should trigger genetic reconsideration.
