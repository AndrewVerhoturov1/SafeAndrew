# Basilisco 2024 — adult CIPO practical guide — русский разбор

## 1. Confirm syndrome before etiology

CIPO requires recurrent obstructive syndrome + bowel dilation/air-fluid levels **without a mechanical occlusive lesion**.

## 2. Then classify etiologic subgroup

Clinically meaningful branches include:

- autoimmune/inflammatory;
- genetically determined visceral neuropathy/myopathy;
- mitochondrial/systemic neuromuscular;
- secondary causes.

## 3. Why subgrouping matters

Different subgroups may have different disease-specific treatment options and different diagnostic tests.

## 4. Avoid surgical anchoring

Mechanical complications can coexist with underlying dysmotility.

Previous surgery/adhesions do not automatically explain persistent nonmechanical transit failure.

## 5. SafeAndrew implication

Patient-specific model should carry **both** layers:

- documented mechanical/stomal complications;
- independent evidence for chronic motor failure without obstruction.

Genetic/immune workup addresses the latter, not every obstruction episode.
