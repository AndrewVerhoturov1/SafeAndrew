# MNGIE International Network 2021 — русский разбор

## 1. Когда classic TYMP-MNGIE plausibly

Сильнее поддерживают сочетание:

- onset usually adolescence/young adulthood;
- severe progressive GI dysmotility;
- cachexia/low body mass;
- sensorimotor peripheral neuropathy;
- ptosis/ophthalmoplegia;
- diffuse leukoencephalopathy.

## 2. Confirmatory pathway

Не останавливаться на `TYMP sequencing negative` при сильном phenotype, если assay/variants uncertain.

Network использует:

- TYMP molecular testing;
- thymidine phosphorylase activity;
- **plasma** thymidine + deoxyuridine.

## 3. Why biochemical confirmation matters

TYMP disease is biochemical: loss of TP causes nucleoside accumulation and mtDNA damage.

Biochemical tests can clarify uncertain sequence results and are highly phenotype-specific in specialist settings.

## 4. Brain MRI

Classic TYMP-MNGIE strongly associated with diffuse leukoencephalopathy.

Absence of this pattern weakens classic TYMP-MNGIE, but does not eliminate broader MNGIE-like disease caused by other genes.

## 5. Neuropathy can mimic CIDP

MNGIE neuropathy can include demyelinating-looking features, so neurogastro phenotype must be considered before labeling immune demyelinating neuropathy.

## 6. Treatment interpretation

Specialized transplant/metabolic interventions are high-risk rare-disease treatments.

Agent may record that **early molecular/biochemical diagnosis can change treatment options**, but must not recommend a procedure outside specialist center.
