# MNGIE GeneReviews — TYMP — русский разбор

## Core phenotype

- severe GI dysmotility;
- cachexia;
- ptosis/ophthalmoplegia;
- sensorimotor neuropathy;
- asymptomatic diffuse leukoencephalopathy.

## Important negative discriminator

GeneReviews прямо указывает: **при отсутствии leukoencephalopathy classic MNGIE disease very unlikely**.

Это сильный discriminator именно для TYMP-classic phenotype, а не для всех mitochondrial neurogastrointestinal diseases.

## Diagnostic confirmation

Один из:

- biallelic TYMP pathogenic variants;
- markedly reduced TP enzyme activity;
- elevated plasma thymidine/deoxyuridine.

## Timing

Symptoms often start before 20 in a substantial proportion, but range first–fifth decade.

## SafeAndrew rule

`severe GI + neuropathy` недостаточно для TYMP diagnosis.

Нужно отдельно проверить:

- brain white matter;
- ophthalmologic phenotype;
- cachexia history;
- molecular/biochemical confirmation.
