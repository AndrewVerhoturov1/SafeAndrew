# RRM2B GeneReviews — MNGIE-like — русский разбор

## Relevant phenotype

Rare biallelic RRM2B disease can produce:

- GI dysmotility;
- cachexia;
- peripheral neuropathy;
- progressive ptosis/ophthalmoplegia.

## Why it matters

It is another proof that a MNGIE-like phenotype can arise from genes beyond TYMP.

## What would increase fit

- ophthalmoplegia/ptosis;
- multisystem mitochondrial signs;
- recessive/de novo-compatible family pattern;
- molecular finding consistent with phenotype.

## Limitation

Very rare and based on small reported case numbers.

Do not elevate RRM2B over broader sequencing solely because GI dysmotility + neuropathy coexist.
