# Fournier & Fabre 2022 — smooth muscle motility genes — русский разбор

## 1. Seven-gene evidence set

Systematic review summarizes ACTG2, MYH11, FLNA, MYLK, RAD21, MYL9, LMOD1.

## 2. Phenotypic discriminator

Among cases with enough phenotype data, combined **CIPO + megacystis** was much more common than isolated CIPO.

Therefore urinary tract phenotype is high-yield information for gene prioritization.

## 3. ACTG2 dominates published cases

ACTG2 accounts for most described patients, but this reflects published rare-disease literature and should not make ACTG2 the only gene tested.

## 4. Adult isolated CIPO caveat

Many published cases are severe pediatric disease. Adult isolated CIPO may have a different gene probability spectrum.

## 5. SafeAndrew rule

Ask genetics to preserve broad smooth-muscle coverage if visceral myopathy branch is supported; don't order one gene solely because it is the most published.
