# MNGIE и MNGIE-like — когда и как проверять

## 1. Classic TYMP-MNGIE

High-fit cluster:

- severe progressive GI dysmotility;
- cachexia;
- sensorimotor neuropathy;
- ptosis/ophthalmoplegia;
- diffuse leukoencephalopathy;
- young onset.

## 2. Strong negative discriminator

Absence of diffuse leukoencephalopathy makes **classic TYMP-MNGIE** very unlikely according to GeneReviews.

This is not equivalent to excluding all MNGIE-like mitochondrial disease.

## 3. TYMP confirmation

Specialist confirmation may use:

- biallelic TYMP variants;
- thymidine phosphorylase activity;
- plasma thymidine/deoxyuridine.

Biochemical testing can be decisive when sequence interpretation is uncertain.

## 4. MNGIE-like beyond TYMP

POLG and RRM2B can produce neurogastrointestinal phenotypes.

POLG is especially important because leukoencephalopathy may be absent.

## 5. Avoid phenotype-by-one-feature

Neither GI dysmotility nor neuropathy alone is enough.

Increase confidence by multisystem constellation and molecular/biochemical confirmation.

## 6. Treatment significance

Some molecularly confirmed MNGIE subtypes can have disease-specific high-risk specialist interventions.

Therefore diagnosis can be medically consequential, but treatment must remain in rare-disease specialist centers.
