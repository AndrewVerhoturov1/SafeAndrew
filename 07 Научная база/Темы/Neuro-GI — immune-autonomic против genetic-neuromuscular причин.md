# Neuro-GI — как разделять immune-autonomic и genetic-neuromuscular причины

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Этап 3. Это причинная карта, а не бинарный тест. Immune и genetic mechanisms могут сосуществовать с postsurgical/mechanical complications.

## 1. Features that shift toward immune-autonomic

- acute/subacute or clearly accelerating onset;
- generalized objective autonomic failure;
- multilevel GI dysmotility plus orthostatic/sudomotor/secretomotor/GU involvement;
- phenotype-matched neural antibody at meaningful level/validated assay;
- systemic autoimmunity/Sjögren clues;
- paraneoplastic high-risk phenotype/antibody context;
- objective improvement with appropriately measured immune-directed treatment in specialist setting.

## 2. Features that shift toward genetic/mitochondrial

- young onset;
- slow multi-year progression;
- neuropathy preceding major surgery;
- multisystem neuro-GI phenotype;
- ophthalmoplegia/ptosis/cachexia/mitochondrial clues;
- family history or de novo/recessive-compatible pattern;
- visceral neuropathy/myopathy on full-thickness pathology;
- bladder smooth-muscle phenotype;
- repeat-expansion/sensory-ataxia phenotype;
- molecular/biochemical confirmation.

## 3. Findings that are weak alone

- one positive autoantibody at low titer;
- one VUS;
- one old report saying `demyelinating`;
- CIPO label without exclusion of mechanical obstruction;
- negative family history;
- negative single-gene TTR;
- negative exome without technology audit;
- subjective response to therapy.

## 4. Objective discriminators

| Question | More useful test/data |
|---|---|
| Generalized autonomic failure? | standardized autonomic reflex screen / CASS |
| Enteric dysfunction objective? | transit/manometry + structural exclusion |
| Immune antibody meaningful? | antigen + assay + titer + phenotype |
| Neuropathy phenotype? | expert EDX/raw review |
| Visceral neuropathy vs myopathy? | expert full-thickness pathology if tissue exists |
| Genetic mechanism? | broad molecular test + CNV/SV/repeat/mitochondrial audit |
| Classic TYMP-MNGIE? | brain MRI phenotype + TYMP/TP activity/plasma nucleosides |

## 5. Do not require one cause

Three models remain valid until evidence separates them:

1. one systemic disease explains neuropathy + GI dysmotility;
2. two independent primary diseases;
3. primary systemic disease plus secondary postsurgical/mechanical/nutritional worsening.

## 6. Priority rule

Do tests that most change the branch:

- expert EDX;
- objective autonomic testing if phenotype supports it;
- broad technology-aware genetics;
- full-thickness pathology re-review if tissue exists.

Broad antibody fishing or repeated non-targeted single-gene testing has lower value.
