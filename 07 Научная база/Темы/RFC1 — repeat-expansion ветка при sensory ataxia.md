# RFC1 — repeat-expansion ветка при sensory ataxia

## Источники

1. [[07 Научная база/Источники/RFC1 GeneReviews 2026 — карточка источника]].
2. [[07 Научная база/Источники/Saporta 2026 — genetic testing peripheral neuropathy — карточка источника]].

## When relevant

More relevant with:

- sensory neuronopathy/predominantly sensory axonal neuropathy;
- sensory ataxia;
- later vestibular/cerebellar findings;
- chronic cough;
- posterior-column abnormalities;
- unexplained late-onset course.

## Technology rule

Most RFC1 disease is due to intronic repeat expansion.

`exome negative` does **not** equal `RFC1 negative`.

## Fit limitations

Classic RFC1 is predominantly late-onset and severe GI pseudo-obstruction is not a core defining feature.

Therefore if neuropathy began much earlier or GI disease dominates, RFC1 should remain a **targeted branch**, not the sole unifying hypothesis.

## Output rule

Future reports must explicitly say whether **repeat-expansion testing** was performed and which motifs/method were covered.
