# Fournier & Fabre 2022 — smooth muscle motility disorder genes — карточка источника

## Паспорт

- **Название:** Smooth muscle motility disorder phenotypes: A systematic review of cases associated with seven pathogenic genes (ACTG2, MYH11, FLNA, MYLK, RAD21, MYL9 and LMOD1)
- **Журнал:** Intractable & Rare Diseases Research
- **Год:** 2022;11(3):113–119
- **DOI:** 10.5582/irdr.2022.01060
- **PMID:** 36200034
- **PMCID:** PMC9437995

## Evidence base

28 articles, 112 patients plus 5 terminated pregnancies.

Distribution:

- ACTG2 67%;
- MYH11 14%;
- FLNA 13%;
- rarer MYLK, RAD21, MYL9, LMOD1.

Among 76 with phenotype data:

- isolated CIPO 13%;
- isolated megacystis 22%;
- combined CIPO + megacystis 63%.

## Why relevant

Shows that smooth-muscle motility genetics is broader than ACTG2 and that bladder phenotype can be a major discriminator.

## Limitation

Case-based rare-disease literature with major ascertainment bias; many severe pediatric phenotypes. Adult-only isolated CIPO requires careful phenotype matching.
