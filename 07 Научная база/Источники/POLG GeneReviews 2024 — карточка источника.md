# POLG-Related Disorders GeneReviews 2024 — карточка источника

## Паспорт

- **GeneReviews:** NBK26471
- **Last update:** 2024-02-29
- **PMID:** 20301791

## Why relevant

POLG spectrum includes:

- peripheral neuropathy, including sensory neuronopathy/ganglionopathy and axonal sensorimotor neuropathy;
- ataxia;
- ptosis/external ophthalmoplegia;
- seizures/migraine/other CNS findings;
- GI dysmotility reported across phenotypes;
- MNGIE-like presentations.

GeneReviews summary reports peripheral neuropathy in ~53% and GI dysmotility across reported POLG phenotypes in roughly 6–52%, depending on phenotype/series.

## Key distinction from classic TYMP-MNGIE

POLG MNGIE-like disease can occur **without leukoencephalopathy**.

Therefore absence of classic TYMP brain MRI pattern does not exclude the wider mitochondrial neurogastrointestinal branch.

## Critical safety point

POLG disorders have important medication-specific safety implications, especially valproate-associated liver risk. This is only relevant if POLG disease becomes a credible/confirmed diagnosis; it is not a reason to change medication independently.
