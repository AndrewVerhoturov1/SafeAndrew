# MNGIE GeneReviews — TYMP — карточка источника

## Паспорт

- **Название:** Mitochondrial Neurogastrointestinal Encephalopathy Disease
- **GeneReviews:** NBK1179
- **Last update:** 2016-01-14
- **PMID:** 20301358

## Suggestive findings

- severe GI dysmotility;
- cachexia;
- ptosis/external ophthalmoplegia;
- sensorimotor neuropathy, often mixed axonal/demyelinating;
- diffuse brain white-matter leukoencephalopathy.

GeneReview states classic TYMP-MNGIE is **very unlikely in absence of leukoencephalopathy**.

## Diagnosis

Can be established by:

- biallelic TYMP pathogenic variants;
- markedly reduced thymidine phosphorylase activity;
- elevated plasma thymidine and deoxyuridine.

## Important mimic lesson

MNGIE neuropathy has been mistaken for CIDP, while GI disease can be misclassified as primary gastrointestinal disease.

## Limitation

GeneReview last updated 2016; use together with newer MNGIE International Network position paper.
