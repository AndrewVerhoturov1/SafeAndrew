# Basilisco et al. 2024 — adult CIPO practical guide — карточка источника

## Паспорт

- **Название:** Chronic intestinal pseudo-obstruction in adults: A practical guide to identify patient subgroups that are suitable for more specific treatments
- **Журнал:** Neurogastroenterology & Motility
- **Год:** 2024;36(1):e14715
- **DOI:** 10.1111/nmo.14715
- **PMID:** 37994282

## Main frame

CIPO is a heterogeneous syndrome: recurrent obstructive symptoms + bowel dilation/air-fluid levels **without mechanical occlusion**.

Practical goal is to identify subgroups where etiology changes management, particularly:

- autoimmune/inflammatory forms;
- genetically determined neuromuscular forms;
- visceral neuropathy/myopathy patterns;
- systemic/mitochondrial conditions.

## Why relevant

Supports moving from syndrome label `CIPO` to etiologic subclassification, rather than repeating decompression/surgery without defining underlying neuromuscular mechanism.

## Limitation

Review/practical guide; treatment subgroup evidence often rare-disease and observational.
