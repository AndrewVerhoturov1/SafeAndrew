# London Classification 2010 — gastrointestinal neuromuscular pathology — карточка источника

## Паспорт

- **Название:** The London Classification of gastrointestinal neuromuscular pathology: report on behalf of the Gastro 2009 International Working Group
- **Журнал:** Gut
- **Год:** 2010;59(7):882–887
- **DOI:** 10.1136/gut.2009.200444
- **PMID:** 20581236

## Purpose

Standardize histopathologic classification/reporting of gastrointestinal neuromuscular disorders in full-thickness tissue.

Core compartments include:

- enteric nerves/ganglia — neuropathy;
- smooth muscle — myopathy;
- interstitial cells of Cajal / related mesenchymal abnormalities;
- mixed/secondary abnormalities.

## Why relevant

If archived full-thickness bowel specimens exist, expert re-review can potentially convert vague `dysmotility/CIPO` into a more specific structural neuromuscular phenotype and guide genetics.

## Limitation

- classification framework, not a genetic diagnostic test;
- tissue handling/staining and site matter;
- absence of a lesion in limited tissue does not exclude functional disease;
- old surgical specimens may have sampling/fixation limitations.
