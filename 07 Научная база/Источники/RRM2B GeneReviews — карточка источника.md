# RRM2B Mitochondrial DNA Maintenance Defects — карточка источника

## Паспорт

- **GeneReviews:** NBK195854
- **Last update:** 2021-06-24
- **PMID:** 24741716

## MNGIE-like phenotype

RRM2B can cause a rare autosomal-recessive MNGIE-like phenotype characterized by:

- progressive ptosis;
- ophthalmoplegia;
- GI dysmotility;
- cachexia;
- peripheral neuropathy.

RRM2B also causes other mitochondrial phenotypes, including childhood encephalomyopathy and adult progressive external ophthalmoplegia.

## Why relevant

RRM2B is a concrete example of why `MNGIE phenotype` is not synonymous with TYMP.

## Limitations

- MNGIE-like RRM2B is rare;
- published case numbers small;
- phenotype overlap is broad;
- relevance requires full clinical/genetic correlation.
