# Bianco et al. 2022 — enteric neuromyopathies genetics — карточка источника

## Паспорт

- **Название:** Enteric Neuromyopathies: Highlights on Genetic Mechanisms Underlying Chronic Intestinal Pseudo-Obstruction
- **Журнал:** Biomolecules
- **Год:** 2022;12(12):1849
- **DOI:** 10.3390/biom12121849
- **PMID:** 36551277
- **PMCID:** PMC9776039

## Mechanistic classes of CIPO

Review organizes severe dysmotility around dysfunction of:

- enteric/extrinsic nerves — neuropathy;
- interstitial cells of Cajal — mesenchymopathy;
- smooth muscle — myopathy;
- mitochondrial/systemic mechanisms.

## Genetic value

Review summarizes multiple genes/mechanisms rather than advocating one universal CIPO panel.

## Why relevant

It provides the bridge between **histopathologic phenotype** and **genetic direction**.

## Limitation

Review spans rare heterogeneous diseases; gene lists must be interpreted through phenotype/pathology and current genetic technology.
