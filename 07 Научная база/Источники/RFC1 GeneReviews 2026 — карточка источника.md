# RFC1-Related Disorders GeneReviews 2026 — карточка источника

## Паспорт

- **GeneReviews:** NBK564656
- **Last update:** 2026-09-22

## Phenotypic spectrum

RFC1 disease ranges from CANVAS to predominantly/isolated sensory neuropathy or neuronopathy, with possible:

- sensory ataxia;
- cerebellar involvement;
- vestibular areflexia;
- chronic cough;
- autonomic dysfunction;
- posterior-column spinal MRI abnormalities.

Motor NCS are usually normal in the classic sensory-predominant spectrum.

## Molecular mechanism

>99% of affected individuals in established cohorts have biallelic **intronic repeat expansions** rather than ordinary coding sequence variants.

Thus a conventional exome or standard sequence-only panel may not detect the common pathogenic mechanism.

## Relevance boundary

RFC1 is predominantly a **late-onset** disorder in classic cohorts. Severe CIPO is not a defining core feature.

Therefore it is a targeted sensory-ataxia/repeat-expansion branch, not automatically the leading unifying explanation of early neuro-GI disease.
