# Deboo et al. 2026 — Sjögren PNS/ANS guideline — карточка источника

## Методология

- systematic review updated through 22.07.2025;
- 31 good-practice statements;
- 20 treatment recommendations;
- GRADE;
- therapeutic evidence base mostly case series/cohorts + expert consensus.

## Особая ценность

Guideline отдельно включает:

- sensory ganglionopathy;
- large/small-fiber neuropathy;
- demyelinating polyradiculoneuropathy;
- autonomic neuropathy/ganglionopathy;
- GI autonomic symptoms;
- formal autonomic testing;
- targeted autoimmune dysautonomia testing in significant autonomic neuropathy.

Good practices для autonomic involvement включают COMPASS-31/postural vitals, formal testing (tilt, QSART, HR variability, Valsalva), CASS, GI motility referral и phenotype-guided autoimmune dysautonomia panel.

## Важная оговорка

Многие treatment recommendations имеют very-low evidence despite strong/conditional wording.
