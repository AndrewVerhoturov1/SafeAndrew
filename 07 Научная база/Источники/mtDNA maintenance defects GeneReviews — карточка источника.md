# Mitochondrial DNA Maintenance Defects Overview — карточка источника

## Паспорт

- **GeneReviews:** NBK487393
- **PMID:** 29517884
- **Posted:** 2018-03-08

## Neurogastrointestinal group

Overview identifies MNGIE/neurogastrointestinal phenotypes caused by nuclear mtDNA-maintenance genes including:

- **TYMP**;
- **POLG**;
- **RRM2B**.

Common unifying manifestations can include:

- GI dysmotility;
- cachexia;
- peripheral neuropathy;
- ophthalmoplegia;
- other multisystem mitochondrial signs.

Important distinction: POLG-related neurogastrointestinal disease can lack the classic TYMP leukoencephalopathy.

## Why source matters

It prevents narrowing the search to TYMP alone when phenotype is MNGIE-like but classic TYMP features are incomplete.
