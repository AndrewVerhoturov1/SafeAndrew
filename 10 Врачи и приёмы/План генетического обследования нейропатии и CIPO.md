# План генетического обследования нейропатии и CIPO

Рабочая patient-specific матрица: [[10 Врачи и приёмы/Генетика neuro-GI — рабочая матрица гипотез]].

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Цель — не заказать одну «панель CMT», а получить genetic strategy, способную проверить **единый neuro-GI диагноз** и при этом не пропустить dual diagnosis.

## 1. С чем идти к neurogenetics

Дать врачу:

- [[10 Врачи и приёмы/ЭНМГ 2017–2026 — рабочая таблица формального пересмотра|рабочую таблицу ЭНМГ 2017–2026]];
- подтверждённую историю multilevel GI dysmotility/CIPO;
- точную surgical anatomy, насколько она известна;
- brain/spinal MRI summaries;
- family history;
- список уже сделанных genetic tests, включая отрицательный `TTR`;
- план pathology re-review, если tissue сохранился.

## 2. Первый вопрос генетику — technology coverage

Нужно письменно понять, покрывает ли выбранный test:

- SNV/indels;
- exon CNV;
- larger structural variants;
- repeat expansions;
- mitochondrial nuclear genes;
- mtDNA where indicated;
- reanalysis pathway;
- difficult regions/known assay blind spots.

Scientific basis: [[07 Научная база/Темы/Генетическая стратегия — нейропатия плюс тяжёлая дисмоторика ЖКТ]].

## 3. Broad sequencing layer

Предпочтение — broad inherited neuropathy + neurogastrointestinal/mitochondrial coverage rather than sequential single-gene guessing.

Конкретно panel/exome/genome выбирает geneticist с учётом laboratory performance.

## 4. Explicit repeat-expansion layer

If sensory-neuronopathy/ataxia phenotype remains strong, explicitly ask whether **RFC1 repeat expansion** was tested.

Do not assume exome covered it.

Current fit caveat: documented neuropathy began relatively young, while classic RFC1 is predominantly late-onset; severe CIPO is not a core RFC1 feature. So RFC1 is targeted, not leading unifying hypothesis.

## 5. MNGIE / MNGIE-like layer

### Classic TYMP-MNGIE

Compare phenotype with:

- severe GI dysmotility;
- cachexia;
- neuropathy;
- ptosis/ophthalmoplegia;
- diffuse leukoencephalopathy.

If classic phenotype becomes convincing, specialist can consider:

- TYMP molecular testing;
- plasma thymidine/deoxyuridine;
- thymidine phosphorylase activity.

If brain MRI lacks diffuse leukoencephalopathy and ophthalmoplegia/cachexia are absent, classic TYMP fit is weaker.

### MNGIE-like

Do not stop at TYMP: POLG and RRM2B are established MNGIE-like branches. POLG can lack leukoencephalopathy.

See [[07 Научная база/Темы/MNGIE и MNGIE-like — когда и как проверять]].

## 6. Visceral myopathy layer

If bowel pathology or bladder phenotype suggests smooth-muscle disease, ensure coverage of ACTG2 and broader smooth-muscle motility genes.

Important distinction:

> ACTG2 can explain CIPO strongly but does not automatically explain generalized peripheral neuropathy.

So any finding must be tested for **unification vs dual diagnosis**.

## 7. Full-thickness pathology

If archived operative bowel tissue exists, ask whether it can be re-reviewed by a GI neuromuscular pathologist.

See [[07 Научная база/Темы/Полнослойная гистология кишечника — что должен оценить эксперт]].

## 8. Family history worksheet

Document in first/second-degree relatives:

- neuropathy/foot deformity;
- ataxia/falls;
- chronic cough/vestibular symptoms;
- GI dysmotility/CIPO/megacolon;
- bladder dysfunction/megacystis;
- ptosis/ophthalmoplegia;
- unexplained cachexia;
- hearing loss;
- mitochondrial/multisystem disease.

Negative family history does not exclude recessive/de novo disease.

## 9. How to handle a result

For each variant/finding record:

1. gene/mechanism;
2. pathogenic/likely pathogenic/VUS;
3. zygosity/inheritance;
4. phenotype match;
5. which part it explains: neuropathy / GI / both;
6. confirmatory biochemical/segregation test if needed;
7. known assay gaps that remain.

## 10. What success looks like

One of:

- **single molecular diagnosis plausibly unifies neuro-GI phenotype**;
- **GI genetic diagnosis + independent neuropathy branch**;
- **neuropathy genetic diagnosis + independent CIPO branch**;
- **no diagnosis, but first-line assay limitations explicitly known and next technology justified**.
